title: "cumTempore"
---

**cumTempore** is a simple clock and timer for keeping time while giving
university lectures or talks. Put your phone or tablet on the lectern, or open
the app on your laptop, and keep an eye on the time.

- **Wall clock:** the local time, as large as your screen allows.
- **Timer:** a countdown in the style of Google's Clock app. The display turns
  dark red during the last five minutes. Once you run over time, the screen
  turns red and the timer counts the overtime.
- **Swipe** to switch between clock and timer.
- The **screen stays on** while the app is open.

The name is Latin: *cum tempore*, "with time", is the origin of the academic
quarter. Lectures announced "c.t." start a quarter of an hour late.

The app has been written by Stefan Kebekus, as a project of [Akaflieg
Freiburg](https://akaflieg-freiburg.de), a flight club based in Freiburg,
Germany. Its clock uses [B612](https://github.com/polarsys/b612), the font that
Airbus designed for aircraft cockpit displays.
